# Murmur design system

Murmur is quiet and dark by default, close to a podcast app: covers do the colour work, one periwinkle accent marks what is selected or playing, and everything else stays out of the way. Follow what already exists. A new screen reuses these tokens and components before it invents anything, on the web and in the Android app alike.

The values below come from [`apps/server/src/web/base.css`](../apps/server/src/web/base.css) (web) and [`apps/mobile/src/theme.ts`](../apps/mobile/src/theme.ts) (app), which are the source of truth. Change a value there and here together.

## Writing

- **Plain words, sentence case.** Titles, buttons and labels are sentence case ("Continue listening", "Add to playlist"). Eyebrow labels are typed in sentence case and uppercased by CSS.
- **Talk to the listener as "you".** "Your music", "Your queue is empty", "48 albums you haven't added". Never "the user", never "I".
- **Name things the way a listener does.** Say "server" or "Downloaded", never "NAS". The music screens never mention the server at all.
- **Controls say exactly what they do, and toasts confirm it.** "Add to Play next", "Add to queue", "Remove from this playlist", then a toast "Removed from the playlist" with Undo. "Delete playlist" is the danger item, and the toast says "Deleted Road trip".
- **Empty states say what's missing and how to fix it.** A bold line, then one muted sentence: "No songs yet" / "Add songs from the menu on any song, with Add to playlist."
- **No dot separators.** Never join metadata as "4 books · Recently added". Use a count pill beside the heading, a second line, or a few pills.
- **Don't repeat what's already on screen.** The sort order lives on the sort button, not in the heading.
- **No emoji.** Numbers use real units: "6h 2m left", "14 of 15 songs", "Most played song, 6 plays".
- **Same words everywhere.** The web and the app use the same labels, chip order and empty states, at every width, even where the web's wide layout places things its own way.

**How to use them.** Build every screen from the same grounds: `bg` for the page, `surface` for raised cards and sheets, `surface-2` for buttons and the mini player, `chip` for chips and pills. `accent` is the only hue in the chrome. `stat-episode`, `stat-music` and `stat-watch` only tell podcasts, music and Watch apart in the stats.

**Covers carry the colour.** A book, show or album header tints its background from the cover, with a blurred copy bleeding out behind it; the full player, the now-playing card and the mini player tint the same way, and on wide screens so do the player bar, Now playing and the top of a book, podcast, movie, TV show or album page. Don't add brand colour around covers.

## Space, shape and depth

- **Space.** The page has a 20px gutter (32px on wide screens, `--page-x`), 28px above the title and 24px above each section. Inside sections use 12px between cards and rows of things, 8px between chips and buttons, 4px between header icons. Every tap target is at least 44px. The mini player is 64px tall; the tab bar is 72px on the web. On wide screens the sidebar is 220px, the player bar 86px tall and the side panel 344px.
- **Corners.** Controls are fully round: chips, pills, buttons, badges, the play button. Covers are 8px in grids, 10px in Continue listening, 12px in headers, 16px in the full player. Inputs are 12px. Cards, menus and the mini player are 14px. Sheets are 20px on top.
- **No borders around cards.** A card is `surface` on `bg`. Lists are rows split by a `line` hairline, not boxed cards.
- **Shadows only on things that float:** the mini player, menus, sheets, the side panel while it floats, a row lifted while dragging (`0 8px 24px` at 45% black dark, 14% light). Header covers get a softer drop shadow. Nothing else casts one.

## Layout

- Phones get a bottom bar: Search, Books, Podcasts, Music, Watch and You. The mini player floats 8px above it.
- **One breakpoint, 900px.** Below it the web is the phone layout and matches the app. From 900px the web has a wide layout of its own, below. CSS decides wherever it can (`@media (min-width: 900px)`); where the two widths need different components, `useWide()` in `web/layout.ts` picks them.
- **App start:** on a cold start the app's mark dances, then its bars land in the M, and the app fades in. The bars keep dancing until the app is ready, and reduced motion shows the M still. The web has no load screen.
- Books, Podcasts, Music and Watch are shown or hidden per person under Tabs, and nothing else hides them; Music and Watch start hidden. When one is hidden, its tab and its parts of You go away, and with Books hidden the app opens on the first tab left. A shown tab with no folder has an empty state; for admins it names the folder to mount (`/music`, or `/movies` and `/shows`), since folders are set up in the compose file.
- **Nested screens:** the back arrow sits alone on the top row, with the title on the row below it, never beside it. A ⋯ menu may share the arrow's row on the right. On wide detail pages that row sits on the cover's colour wash, or tops the left column of a two-column podcast or album page.

### Wide screens

The window doesn't scroll. It's a grid (`shell.css`): the sidebar on the left, the page area in the middle, the side panel on the right while it's open, and the player bar along the bottom under all three while something is loaded.

- **Sidebar** (`.tabs`, 220px, `nav` with a `line` edge). The Murmur mark, the search box, always open, then the shown tabs as rows, icon beside label; the selected row is `accent` on a faint `accent` wash. "/" pressed outside a field jumps to the box, with a small "/" key hint in it until it's focused (see Search).
- **Page area** (`main.page`). Fills the width between sidebar and panel, with no column cap, and scrolls on its own while the rest stays put. Pages lay themselves out by the page area's width rather than the window's (`container: page`), so the side panel taking room changes a page the same way a narrower window does; two-column pages split once there's 840px of content. A new page opens at the top and takes keyboard focus, so Page Down, Space and the arrows scroll it straight away; Back and Forward put it back where it was scrolled. You and its settings screens stay at most 720px wide.
- **Player bar** (`PlayerBar`, 86px). Takes the mini player's place, docked edge to edge along the bottom (see Player bar).
- **Side panel** (`SidePanel`, 344px, `nav` with a `line` edge). Holds the queue, chapters and bookmarks that phones open as sheets. From 1200px it docks beside the page, which gets narrower, and starts open; from 900 to 1199px it floats over the right of the page with a shadow, starts closed, and a click outside it or Escape closes it. Whether it's open, and on which tab, is remembered per browser (`murmur.sidePanel`). It only shows while something is loaded. Text tabs Queue / Chapters / Bookmarks sit along its top with an X; a book or episode without chapters has no Chapters tab, and songs get the queue alone, titled "Queue" with its grey count, the "From …" line and Clear. Under the tabs, the queue starts with a line instead: "From …" (or "Up next"), the grey count and Clear. Chapters and bookmarks picked there play without closing it.
- **Now playing** (`NowPlaying`). Opening the player shows Now playing inside the page area, over the page, which keeps its place underneath; the sidebar, panel and bar stay. It's tinted like the full player: a top row with a down arrow on the left and the cast button and ⋮ on the right, then the cover beside the text once there's room (stacked when the area is narrow): "Playing from …", the title with the bookmark button, a book's chapter segments and chapter line, pills (time left, chapter N of M), and the tiles; books and episodes add their Bookmarks under it. It has no transport or scrubber of its own: the bar has them. The down arrow, the bar's expand button, Escape, a sidebar tab, typing in the search box, or going to another page closes it. There's no full-window audio player on wide screens.
- **Video.** The player page takes the page area too: the picture full width at 16:9 along the top (smaller and centred on a short window, so the title stays in view), then "Playing from …" with the cast button and ⋮, the title, the show, pills (the episode or version, time left) and the description. A click on the picture plays or pauses, a double click goes full screen. Its controls are in the bar (see Player bar) and its next episodes in a Next episodes panel on the right, the side panel's size and modes, with season text tabs when they span more than one season; whether it's open is remembered per browser (`murmur.videoPanel`). Minimise goes back to the page you came from and docks the same picture, still playing, as a small thumbnail at the bar's left; a click on it opens the player again. Full screen is the same at every width.

## Motion and states

- Short and functional: pages, and views switched by text tabs, fade in over 220ms, sheets and the player rise 24px over about 200ms, covers fade in once loaded, dragged rows slide out of the way. On wide screens Now playing and the side panel (and the view under each of its tabs) fade in place over about 200ms instead of rising. All of it stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- On the app and the web at phone width, the full player minimises like a sheet: a pull down that starts on its top bar or cover moves it with the finger, showing the screen below, and closes it past a quarter of the screen (120 points on the app) or on a quick flick; otherwise it springs back. A tap on the bar's buttons still works. Below the cover, drags stay with the slider and controls.
- Focus is a 2px `accent` outline, offset 2px. Disabled is 45% opacity. Hover on icon buttons is a 7% `text` wash.
- Selected chips switch to `accent` with `on-accent` text; a selected text tab turns `text` over an `accent` underline. The playing row's title turns `accent`.

## Icons

Murmur draws its own line icons in the Lucide style: 24px viewBox, 2px round strokes, no fill, `currentColor`. Play and pause are drawn solid. They live in [`apps/server/src/web/icons.tsx`](../apps/server/src/web/icons.tsx), and the app copies the same paths in `apps/mobile/src/components/icons.tsx`. Use 24px in the tab bar and most buttons, and 18 to 22px inside buttons with words. Reuse an existing icon before drawing a new one, and never use emoji as icons.

## Logo

[`apps/server/src/app/icon.svg`](../apps/server/src/app/icon.svg) is the mark: five rounded bars in `#8e9bff` on a `#141833` rounded square. It's the browser icon and sits beside "Murmur" (22 / 800) at the top of the wide-screen sidebar. `apps/mobile/assets/icon.png` is the Android launcher icon. Don't recolour or redraw either.

## Components

Web class names are in a stylesheet beside each feature (`web/player/player.css`, `web/watch/watch.css`), loaded in order by `app/globals.css`; the app has matching components under `apps/mobile/src/components/`. On the web, use the React components in [`apps/server/src/web/ui.tsx`](../apps/server/src/web/ui.tsx) where one exists instead of writing the class names out, and add a new one there when a pattern repeats.

- **Button** (`.btn`, `.btn-primary`, `.btn-big`, `.btn-round`, `.btn-outline`, `.btn-small`, `.btn-danger`). One primary big button per screen (Play, Continue, Make playlist), usually with a round Shuffle beside it. While that book, album or playlist is the one playing, Play reads Pause, or Resume when paused. Starting it opens the full player (Now playing on wide screens); Pause and Resume stay on the page. Plain buttons for secondary actions, outline for a quiet add action under a list. Labels are verbs that say what happens, never "OK" or "Submit".
- **Icon button** (`.icon-btn`). 44px round, one 24px icon, an `aria-label`. For search, sort, ⋯ and the back arrow. Use a text button when no familiar icon says the action.
- **Back** (`BackRow`, `NestedHead`, `HeroBar`). The arrow goes back, or to a fallback screen when the page was opened directly. `NestedHead` is a nested screen's whole head: the back row, then the title with its count, a sub line and actions on the right. `HeroBar` is the top row of a page with a hero (a book, show or movie): the arrow, and ⋯ on the right.
- **Text tabs** (`Chips kind="tabs"`, `.subtabs`; `TextTabs` in the app). Switch between views of the same content: Home / Authors / Series / Finished, Home / Playlists / Albums / Artists / Songs, Home / Movies / Shows / Watched, Week / Month / Year / All time, Chapters / Bookmarks (in the sheet and in a book page's wide column), Queue / Chapters / Bookmarks in the side panel, All / Not added / New in Browse, Add songs' sources, Search's kinds, a show's seasons, and the tabs in edit sheets. Every view switch is a text tab; chips are only for settings. Labels are 15 / 700 in `muted`; the selected one is `text` with a 2px `accent` underline over a `line` hairline. They sit under the page title, 24px apart, and scroll sideways when they don't fit. The underline slides to the tab picked (about 280ms; already in place when the page opens), and the view under the tabs fades in. One selected, never an action.
- **Chip** (`Chips`, `Chip`; `.chip`, `.chips`). Picks one setting (`radio`: Theme, Skip back, a podcast's ads) or turns fields on and off one by one (`toggle`: the fields Match all takes). Never switches views; that's a text tab. Admin lists (Needs attention, Not matched) aren't chips: they're rows in You > Library.
- **Badge** (`CountPill`, `SectionTitle`; `.count-pill`, `.badge-count`, `.new-pill`). Grey counts what's in a list; accent flags something new or waiting. No count next to a screen's title, nested or not. `SectionTitle` is a section heading with its count, as a button when it opens the full list.
- **Search** (`SearchView`; `app/(tabs)/search.tsx` in the app). One search for everything, with one box at every width. On phones it's opened from the Search tab: the box grows out of the tab across the bar (see Bottom bar), focused, with an `accent` ring and an X that goes back. On wide screens the box is always open near the top of the sidebar, on `surface` with a `line` edge that turns `accent` on focus, and a small "/" key hint at its end until it's focused; "/" pressed outside a field jumps to it, typing or Enter opens the search page, and Escape leaves the box. Text tabs All / Books / Podcasts / Music / Watch sit above the results, one for each kind that found something (and only shown sections); with no query or nothing found there are no tabs, and a tab that empties goes back to All. All shows each group (Books, Authors, Series, Podcasts, Episodes, Artists, Albums, Songs, Movies and shows, TV episodes) as a shelf of its usual cards, or 4 of its usual rows, under a heading that opens its kind's tab; a kind's tab shows grids and full lists. No count pills: the groups are capped. With the box empty, a Recent heading lists the last 8 searches, each with an X; a search is kept when it's submitted or a result is opened. Offline, the app searches what's downloaded and says "Showing downloaded only". No results reads `Nothing matches "<query>".` everywhere.
- **Search box** (`SearchBox`). For finding inside one screen's own list (Browse, Add songs, the podcast directory, a show's episodes): opens under the page head, focused, with a placeholder that says what it searches.
- **Pill** (`.pill`, `.pills`). Two or three short facts in a header (1977, 11 songs, 39m) instead of a dotted string. Not clickable. A pill can lead with a small accent icon, like the lock on a private show's Private pill.
- **Cover** (`Cover` in `web/cover.tsx`). Square artwork with a tinted fallback that shows the title. Artists are round; playlists with four or more albums show a 2×2 grid. No borders, no text over real artwork.
- **Card** (`.card`, `.grid`, `.shelf`). A cover with a title and one sub line, straight on `bg`. A started book adds a progress bar; a finished one gets the accent check. Books, albums, podcasts and playlists share one size: grids are 3 across and shelves 2.5 across on phones. On wide screens cards are about 140 to 170px: a grid fits as many across as its width takes, and a shelf shows a whole number of cards edge to edge (4 to 10, by the shelf's own width), so its arrows page by whole cards; 16:9 stills get 260px columns. Continue listening cards are about a quarter bigger and show the title, author and time left. On wide screens Continue rows wrap into a grid instead of scrolling: books' and episodes' are wide `surface` cards (at least 300px) with the cover on the left, the title, author or show, a progress bar and time left, and a round play button on the right that starts it (opening Now playing, like Play) or pauses and resumes it if it's the one loaded; Continue watching's 16:9 cards wrap the same way (at least 190px). Book, album, movie and show cards all open their item's menu the same way (`CardMenu` in `web/card-menu.tsx`): ⋯ in the top right corner or a right-click on the web, a long press with a haptic in the app. Admin items come last after a divider and say Edit… and Match…. The ⋯ on the item's page opens the same items in a sheet (`MenuSheet`), for everyone.
- **Scroll row** (`ScrollRow`, web only). Wraps a shelf so a mouse gets round arrows at its ends while hovering, each hidden once that end is reached. Touch screens just swipe.
- **Shelves.** A section title over a shelf of cards; an empty shelf isn't shown at all, and one that can't be built is left off without touching the rest of the page. "More like" names its item ("More like Perelandra"); Customize lists it under that title too. Short listens' cards show the length as their sub line. Music Home stacks Jump back in (album and playlist cards), On repeat (five song rows; picking one queues the whole list of up to 25), More like *album* (album cards with the add button), More like *artist* (artist cards: a round photo, else one of their covers, else a person icon, with the name and album count centred under it, `.artist-card`) and New albums (album cards with the add button). On a movie page the collection shelf marks the current film with an accent title (`.card-current`).
- **Detail pages on wide screens** (`.detail`). A book, podcast, movie, TV show, album or playlist page washes the top of the page area with the cover's colour (a playlist's is the plain page tint), edge to edge, fading out over about 480px; a movie or show with a backdrop shows it there instead of the blurred cover. Once there's 840px of content they split into two columns:
  - Book: the cover, details, actions and description on the left; on the right a `surface` column with Chapters and Bookmarks text tabs that stays in view and scrolls inside itself. In one column both sections follow the description under their headings, as on phones.
  - Podcast and album: the cover full width on the left with the title, pills and actions under it, staying in view; the episodes, or the numbered songs, on the right, starting level with the cover. A podcast's episodes head turns solid only once it sticks. An album that's all in your music gets a round `accent` check after Shuffle that takes it out again.
  - Movie and TV show: the hero and actions on the left, the settings card (Version, Audio, Subtitles) on the right, level with the bottom of the hero, and the rest full width under them. A show's episodes go two to a row where they fit, with 160px stills.
  - Playlist: the cover with the title, pills and buttons beside it, Add songs with its label as an outline pill, and the column heads over the songs.
- **Subtitle line** (`.vp-subs`). White 600 text on a 62% black box with 6px corners, one box per line, centred at the bottom of the picture and sized to it (13px on a phone held upright, about 20px sideways). It moves up above the controls when they show. The Subtitles tile fills with `accent` while subtitles are on.
- **New tile** (`.new-tile`). Making something new from a grid is its first tile: a cover-sized `surface-2` square (the cover placeholder colour, so it reads in both themes) with an accent plus and no outline, its name below as the card title. Used for New playlist.
- **Mini player sub line** (`.mini-split`). A book's chapter and time left share one line; the chapter name gives way first. In the player bar they take two lines.
- **List row** (`.list-row`, `.queue-row`; `SongRow` for songs). Thumbnail, bold title, one muted sub line, trailing controls (length, ⋯ menu, drag handle). The left part is the tap target. The playing row's title is `accent`. Lists dragged into order use `useSortable` and its `DragHandle` (in `web/sortable.tsx`), which also moves a row with the arrow keys.
  - **Song rows on wide screens** lay out by the row's own width, so they work beside the side panel too. The length is a right-aligned tabular column and an Add to queue icon button sits before the ⋯ (`accent` while queued, off for the song playing). From 700px of row, an Album column (30%, a link to the album) appears and the sub line is the artist alone instead of "Artist, Album".
  - **Playlist column heads** (`.song-head`, wide screens only). Title, Album and Length over the rows, lined up with their columns. Each sorts the list A to Z (shortest first for Length) with an `accent` arrow, a second click reverses it and a third goes back to the playlist's own order (`sortSongs` in shared). The sort isn't saved; while sorted the drag handles hide, and Play, Shuffle and a row play in the order shown. Phones always show the playlist's own order.
- **Track list** (`.track-list`, `.chapter-list`). Numbered rows for an album's songs or a book's chapters, numbers right-aligned and tabular, no thumbnails. On wide screens an album's rows add the Add to queue button.
- **Mini player** (`.mini`, phones only). Cover, title, sub line, transport and a 2px accent progress line. Books and episodes get skip back 10 / forward 30; songs get previous / next, never speed or skip-seconds. From 900px the player bar takes its place.
- **Player bar** (`PlayerBar`, `.pbar`; wide screens). Docked along the bottom, 86px, tinted from the cover like the mini player, in three parts. Left: the cover, title and sub line (a book's chapter and time left), which open Now playing. Middle: the player controls, smaller, with a light play disc, over the scrubber with the elapsed time and time left either side. Right: a speed pill (books and episodes), Sleep (`accent` while set), the bookmark button (books and episodes), mute and a volume slider, a divider, then the Chapters (or Bookmarks) and Queue buttons that open the side panel on that tab and turn `accent` while it's showing, and a round expand button: an up arrow that opens Now playing, `accent` with a down arrow while it's open. Songs never get speed or skip-seconds. Under 1100px the volume slider and Sleep drop out first; mute stays. For a video the bar carries its controls: the thumbnail, title, show and "S1 E1, 1m left" on the left; Start over, skip back, play, skip forward and next episode over the scrubber; then Subtitles (`accent` while on, only with subtitles), Audio (more than one track), mute and volume, a divider, Next episodes (only with any), picture in picture, full screen and the expand button, which minimises the player or opens it again.
- **Bottom bar** (`.tabs`, `DockSearch`). Search, then the shown tabs in a fixed order, sharing the width equally, each a 24px icon over an 11px label; the selected one is `accent`. Tapping Search grows its box out of the tab across the bar over about 300ms while the other tabs give up their width and fade, and an X slides in at the end; the keyboard opens with it. The X, Escape or Back runs it in reverse. It starts on the tap, not when the page has changed. Settings live on You, not in the bar. From 900px the same tabs and box make up the sidebar (see Layout).
- **Sheet** (`Sheet`, `SheetItem` in `web/sheet.tsx`). Rises from the bottom for item menus, one- or two-field forms and lists like the queue. Items are verbs; a danger item goes last. Rows have no lines between them, only a highlight on hover, press or keyboard focus (no focus ring), and the content scrolls without a scrollbar; the one divider sits before admin items. Close it before acting and confirm with a toast. No browser dialogs. A sheet can carry a grey count, a sub line and one action (like Clear) in its title row; the title row stays put and only the content under it scrolls, at most 85% of the screen tall. On wide screens the queue, chapters and bookmarks open in the side panel instead; item menus, forms, Sleep and a bookmark's note are still sheets.
- **Player controls** (`.player-controls`). Books and episodes: previous chapter, skip back, play, skip forward, next chapter. An episode without chapters (most of them) uses the outer pair for previous and next instead: the item before or after it in the queue, then the episode above or below it in its show's list. Songs: Repeat, previous, play, next, Shuffle. Shuffle and Repeat are muted icon buttons that turn `accent` when on; Repeat one adds a small 1 to the loop. One `Transport` component serves the full player and the player bar, where it's smaller with a light play disc.
- **Playing mixer** (`Mixer`, `.mixer`). Five accent bars bouncing out of step on a dark disc, centered on the cover of the book, album, playlist or show that's playing; the card's title turns `accent`. Paused, the bars ease from where they were into the app icon's shape (full, ¾, about half, ¾, full) and stay there. The playing song's thumbnail in playlist and Songs rows gets the thumbnail version. In an album's track list, the bars alone (`MixerBars`, accent, no disc) take the playing song's number. On the playing row in the queue it fills the thumbnail and shows Play while paused, since tapping that row pauses and resumes.
- **Queue rows** (`web/up-next.tsx`). One running list: played rows in `faint` with a dimmed thumbnail, the playing row with the mixer and an `accent` title, then what's next. Each row: thumbnail, title (`Marquee`: scrolls sideways when it doesn't fit, ellipsis with reduced motion), artist or show with the length beside it, then X and the drag handle. No section labels. Reduced motion stops the bars. The same list fills the queue sheet and the side panel's Queue, each opening on the playing row.
- **Volume row** (`VolumeRow`, `.vol-row`). The web full player's volume with a mouse, under the controls: a mute button showing the soft speaker, a slider, and the loud speaker at the far end, so the title keeps its full width. Hidden on touch screens. The video player below 900px keeps its speaker button that slides a slider out on hover. On wide screens the player bar has a mute button and an 84px slider instead (`VolumeBar`), for audio and video alike; the slider hides on touch screens and under 1100px.
- **Player tiles** (`.tile`). Speed, Sleep, a third slot, and Queue with a grey count; each opens its control in place or in a sheet. The slot (`thirdTile` in shared) is Chapters for anything with more than one chapter, else Bookmarks. Chapters' sheet has Chapters and Bookmarks text tabs, and its title and grey count follow the tab. On wide screens the tiles sit in Now playing, and Chapters, Bookmarks and Queue open the side panel on their tab instead of a sheet, staying `accent` while that tab shows; Speed and Sleep work as on phones. The order never changes, and the slot is decided when the item loads so nothing pops in mid-listen. The ad setting sits in the episode's ⋯ menu as a row with its value and a chevron.
- **Bookmark rows** (`BookmarkRows`, `.bookmark`). One row everywhere (the player's sheet, the side panel, Now playing's Bookmarks, the book page and an opened episode row): a round `surface-2` play button, the time as the title (15 / 700, tabular), the chapter if there is one, the note if there is one in `text-2`, and a pencil. No "No note" text. Sorted by time. An episode row with bookmarks gets a small `accent` bookmark beside its date, as `.ads-checked` does. The bookmark beside the player's title fills within 15 seconds of one. On the scrubber they're `accent` ticks taller than the bar (`.scrub-bookmarks`), over played, unplayed and ad marks alike and under the thumb.
- **Chapter scrubber** (`.scrub-segs`, `.scrub-seg`). An episode with chapters gets one segment per chapter across the whole bar, with small gaps between: played ones solid, the current one filling, the rest faint, with ad marks on top. The playing chapter's title takes the middle of the times row in place of the time left (`.player-chapter-line`, 12 / 700, ellipsis; tap for the list). While dragging, the current segment thickens and the times row shows the title of the chapter under the thumb in `accent`. On the web, hovering with a mouse does the same for the spot under the pointer, and a later chapter's segment lights up in `accent` (`.scrub-seg.hovered`). A book's bar spans its current chapter, with the chapter's name as the line under the title (the Chapters tile opens the list). In the player bar the times sit either side of the scrubber with no chapter line between them; the bar's sub line and Now playing name the chapter.
- **Chapter list** (`ChapterList`, `.chapter-list`). Number, title on one line with an ellipsis, and length. Played rows are muted, and the current row has an `accent` title, with `MixerBars` in place of its number. The sheet title carries a grey count. The side panel's Chapters and the book page's wide column show the same list. Video has no speed: its tiles are Audio, Pop out and Full screen (buttons in the player bar on wide screens).
- **Floating card** (`.float-card`). A short note with one `accent` text action and an X, floating over a player so nothing moves when it comes and goes; it fades in and stays dismissed until what it's about changes. Used for the video player's "Converting slower than it plays", just above the bottom tiles, and the audio player's Go back after a jump to a bookmark, just below the top bar, where it can't cover the controls on a short screen. On wide screens Go back sits at the top of Now playing, between its top row's buttons, and the video's card sits over the bottom left of the picture. Anything that stays up while you listen on the audio player uses a cover pill instead, since a phone has little room there.
- **Cover pill** (`.skip-ad`, `.finish-pill`). A small pill in the cover's bottom-right corner for a timely one-tap action, so the controls never move: Skip ad (ad colours) while an ad plays, and Mark played (`accent`, with a check; both icons sit after the text) in an episode's last 3 minutes. When both apply they stack (`.cover-pills`), Mark played on top. Skip ad goes once less than a second is left, since an ad that runs to the end has nothing to skip to.
- **Player top row** (`.player-top`). Close on the left, the "Playing from Books" eyebrow centred ("Finished" once a book has stopped at its end), and the cast button and ⋮ menu on the right. Now playing keeps the row's buttons and moves the eyebrow over the title, left-aligned.
- **Cast button** (`CastButton`). Google's cast mark, only there when a Chromecast is found; `accent` with its screen filled while casting. While casting, the eyebrow reads "Playing on Living Room", the mini player's sub line is a small accent cast mark and the device name, and the video stage shows the backdrop at 45% with a white cast mark.
- **Settings card** (`SettingsCard`, `Setting`, `SettingSub`, `SettingLink`, `SwitchRow`, `StatusDot`; `.card-box`, `.setting`). Rows with a title, optional sub line, and a value with chevron, a switch or a button. One setting per row. A status dot before the title is green when fine, amber when it needs a look. Rescan buttons always carry the refresh icon before the word, and it spins while the scan runs.
- **Choice sheet** (`ChoiceSheet`, web). A sheet of a few choices, like a sort. The current one is marked, and picking closes it.
- **Confirm sheet** (`ConfirmSheet`, web and app). Asks before something can't be undone: a title ending in a question mark ("Delete Road trip?"), one line on what happens, then Cancel and the action: danger for removing something, primary for signing out. Used for signing out, removing a user, deleting a playlist and unsubscribing.
- **Form controls** (`.input`, `.switch`, checkboxes). 44px inputs on `surface` with a `line` border that turns `accent` on focus. Placeholders say what to type ("Playlist name"). Each form has one primary button.
- **Stats** (`StatsCard`, `StatsPage`, `.stats-*`; the rules are in `stats-page.ts` in shared). On You, one card: this week's total and comparison, a chevron and a sparkline. The card opens Stats, a nested screen with Week, Month, Year and All time text tabs, each fading in.
  - **Sparkline** (`.stats-spark`). One `accent` line with a faint fill and a dot at today, and a `track` line for the days to come. Under it (`.stats-axis`), the first and last day, and Month's 15th, in small `muted` text, and Today in `text`, which takes the place of any label it would crowd. Month's is a rolling average.
  - **Sentences.** The lead is Stats total type with the main kind in its colour. Week's rows are the day, a cover, what you played most and the time, with Today in `accent`. Month's are a cover, a sentence in 16 / 700 and a chevron. Rows part with `line` hairlines.
  - **Ring** (`.stats-ring`). A spoke per day, 2.2 wide with round caps, in `stat-book` (the accent), `stat-episode`, `stat-music` or `stat-watch` by the week's main kind; `track` stubs for days with nothing played and days to come; a `text` line at today; the total in the middle and Jan, Apr, Jul and Oct around it, up to 440 wide. Year has the legend below it, which is also the filter: picking a kind outlines it in its colour, fades the others and turns the ring, total and Top to that kind. All time has year tiles below it instead (`.stats-years`): 44 high, sharing the row and scrolling sideways once there are too many to fit, with the picked one in `accent`.
  - **Top** (`.stats-top-strip`). The period's top things as square covers with the kind as a small uppercase label, scrolling sideways on a phone and a grid with room.
  - A hidden tab leaves its kind out everywhere, the total included; with only books left there's no legend. With no listening at all, the screen is just its empty state.
- **Episode rows** (`.episode-row`). A 16:9 still with a progress bar, then the title over "S1 E2, 31m" in a smaller muted line, both sitting on the still's bottom edge, and a watched toggle on the right. The video player's Next episodes list uses the same row and scrolls with a thin bar and no arrows; on short windows the picture shrinks so a row and a half still show. On wide screens that list is the Next episodes panel (see Layout), where a watched toggle greys the row with a toast and Undo.
- **Podcast episode rows** (`EpisodeRow`, `.episode`). On phones: the date (and the show, on lists across shows) over the title and a one-line description that opens the notes in place, then a play pill with the length, a progress bar once started, and the queue and played buttons. From 900px each episode is one line in columns: the thumbnail (lists across shows), the title on one line over the show's name or the description, the date, the length (progress bar under it), then the queue and played buttons and a round play button. Opened notes and bookmarks go on a row underneath.
- **Browse row** (`.browse-row`). A full-width `surface` row with an icon tile, title, sub line, optional "N new" pill and chevron, leading to the rest of a catalogue. One per screen.
- **Empty state** (`EmptyState`; `.empty`, `.music-empty`). One bold line and one muted sentence, no illustrations, no blame.
