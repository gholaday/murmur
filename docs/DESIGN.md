# Murmur design system

Murmur is quiet and dark by default, close to a podcast app: covers do the colour work, one periwinkle accent marks what is selected or playing, and everything else stays out of the way. Follow what already exists. A new screen reuses these tokens and components before it invents anything, on the web and in the Android app alike.

The values below come from [`apps/server/src/web/base.css`](../apps/server/src/web/base.css) (web) and [`apps/mobile/src/theme.ts`](../apps/mobile/src/theme.ts) (app), which are the source of truth. Change a value there and here together.

## Writing

- **Plain words, sentence case.** Titles, buttons and labels are sentence case ("Continue listening", "Add to playlist"). Eyebrow labels are typed in sentence case and uppercased by CSS.
- **Talk to the listener as "you".** "Your music", "Your queue is empty", "48 albums you haven't added". Never "the user", never "I".
- **Name things the way a listener does.** Say "server" or "Downloaded", never "NAS". The music screens never mention the server at all.
- **Controls say exactly what they do, and toasts confirm it.** "Add to Play next", "Add to queue", "Remove from this playlist", then a toast "Removed from the playlist" with Undo. "Delete playlist" is the danger item, and the toast says "Deleted Road trip".
- **Empty states say what's missing and how to fix it.** A bold line, then one muted sentence: "No songs yet" / "Add songs from the menu on any song, with Add to playlist."
- **No dot separators.** Never join metadata as "4 books · Recently added". Use a count pill beside the heading, a second line, or one muted facts line spaced apart (`.book-meta`).
- **Don't repeat what's already on screen.** The sort order lives on the sort button, not in the heading.
- **No emoji.** Numbers use real units: "6h 2m left", "14 of 15 songs", "Most played song, 6 plays".
- **Same words everywhere.** The web and the app use the same labels, chip order and empty states.

Fixed colours used in both themes: `#5ccf8a` success and `#f0b45a` warning (status dots, match scores; both read poorly on white, so pair them with words), and the ad colours `#ef9f27`, `#ba7517` (already played) and `#412402` (text on the Skip ad button).

**How to use them.** Build every screen from the same grounds: `bg` for the page, `surface` for raised cards and sheets, `surface-2` for buttons and the mini player, `chip` for chips and pills. `accent` is the only hue in the chrome. `stat-episode`, `stat-music` and `stat-watch` only tell podcasts, music and Watch apart in the stats.

**Covers carry the colour.** A book, show or album header tints its background from the cover, with a blurred copy bleeding out behind it; the full player, the now-playing card and the mini player tint the same way. Don't add brand colour around covers.

## Space, shape and depth

- **Space.** The page has a 20px gutter, 28px above the title and 24px above each section. Inside sections use 12px between cards and rows of things, 8px between chips and buttons, 4px between header icons. Every tap target is at least 44px. The mini player is 64px tall; the tab bar is 72px on the web.
- **Corners.** Controls are fully round: chips, pills, buttons, badges, the play button. Covers are 8px in grids, 10px in Continue listening, 12px in headers, 16px in the full player. Inputs are 12px. Cards, menus and the mini player are 14px. Sheets are 20px on top.
- **No borders around cards.** A card is `surface` on `bg`. Lists are rows split by a `line` hairline, not boxed cards.
- **Shadows only on things that float:** the mini player, menus, sheets, a row lifted while dragging (`0 8px 24px` at 45% black dark, 14% light). Header covers get a softer drop shadow. Nothing else casts one.

## Layout

- Phones get a bottom bar: Search, Library, Podcasts and You. The mini player floats 8px above it. Library holds Books, Music and Watch as sections (see Library title).
- From 900px wide the bar becomes a 220px sidebar with the Murmur mark and Search as its first item, and the page is a centred column of at most 1080px.
- **App start:** on a cold start the app's mark dances, then its bars land in the M, and the app fades in. The bars keep dancing until the app is ready, and reduced motion shows the M still. The web has no load screen.
- Books, Music, Watch and Podcasts are shown or hidden per person under "Choose what you see" (first run, and You > Appearance), and nothing else hides them; Music and Watch start hidden. When one is hidden, it leaves Library or the bar and its parts of You go away; with all three Library sections hidden, Library goes. The app opens on Library's first section, else Podcasts, else You. A shown tab with no folder has an empty state; for admins it names the folder to mount (`/music`, or `/movies` and `/shows`), since folders are set up in the compose file.
- **Nested screens:** the back arrow, the title and the action icons share one row, as on pages with a hero. A long title stays on one line and ends in "…".
- **First run** (`/welcome`, `Welcome` in `web/welcome/`; `onboardingSteps` in shared). Shown once per person after the first sign-in (`prefs.onboarded`), before anything else. One centred column with no tab bar: the mark and "Murmur" top left and Skip as accent text top right, then "2 of 4" as an eyebrow, the step's title (28 / 800) and one muted line, flat settings rows, and a full-width big primary Continue at the foot ("Start listening" on the last step, which has no Skip). Steps: Your library (admins: each kind's folders and scan progress), Choose what you see (On/Off rows), Your podcasts (Import, Find shows), and on the web Listen on your phone (the Android app download and the server address). Finishing or skipping goes to the library and never comes back.

## Motion and states

- Short and functional: pages, and views switched by text tabs, fade in over 220ms, sheets and the player rise 24px over about 200ms (and a sheet sinks away again as it closes, its backdrop fading with it), covers fade in once loaded, dragged rows slide out of the way. All of it stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- On the app and the web at phone width, the full player minimises like a sheet: a pull down that starts on its top bar or cover moves it with the finger, showing the screen below, and closes it past a quarter of the screen (120 points on the app) or on a quick flick; otherwise it springs back. A tap on the bar's buttons still works. Below the cover, drags stay with the slider and controls.
- Focus is a 2px `accent` outline, offset 2px. Disabled is 45% opacity. Hover on icon buttons is a 7% `text` wash.
- Selected chips switch to `accent` with `on-accent` text; a selected text tab turns `text` over an `accent` underline. The playing row's title turns `accent`.

## Icons

Murmur draws its own line icons in the Lucide style: 24px viewBox, 2px round strokes, no fill, `currentColor`. Play and pause are drawn solid. They live in [`apps/server/src/web/icons.tsx`](../apps/server/src/web/icons.tsx), and the app copies the same paths in `apps/mobile/src/components/icons.tsx`. Use 24px in the tab bar and most buttons, and 18 to 22px inside buttons with words. Reuse an existing icon before drawing a new one, and never use emoji as icons.

## Logo

[`apps/server/src/app/icon.svg`](../apps/server/src/app/icon.svg) is the mark: five rounded bars in `#8e9bff` on a `#141833` rounded square. It's the browser icon and sits beside "Murmur" (22 / 800) at the top of the wide-screen sidebar. `apps/mobile/assets/icon.png` is the Android launcher icon. Don't recolour or redraw either.

## Components

Web class names are in a stylesheet beside each feature (`web/player/player.css`, `web/watch/watch.css`), loaded in order by `app/globals.css`; the app has matching components under `apps/mobile/src/components/`. On the web, use the React components in [`apps/server/src/web/ui.tsx`](../apps/server/src/web/ui.tsx) where one exists instead of writing the class names out, and add a new one there when a pattern repeats.

- **Button** (`.btn`, `.btn-primary`, `.btn-big`, `.btn-round`, `.btn-outline`, `.btn-small`, `.btn-danger`, `.btn-danger-fill`). One primary big button per screen (Play, Continue, Make playlist), usually with a round Shuffle beside it. While that book, album or playlist is the one playing, Play reads Pause, or Resume when paused. Starting it opens the full player; Pause and Resume stay on the page. Plain buttons for secondary actions, outline for a quiet add action under a list. `.btn-danger-fill` (solid `danger`, `bg`-coloured text) is only a confirm's action when it removes something. Labels are verbs that say what happens, never "OK" or "Submit".
- **Text action** (`.text-btn`; a settings row's `.btn`). An action said in `accent` text (14 / 700) with its 18px icon before the word and no pill, washed with 10% `accent` on hover; `.danger` makes it red. Used for a select bar's actions (without their icons on wide screens), settings rows (Rescan, Change, Export, Download, Copy, Retry, Add), Skip on first run, and "Use the files' cover". A toast's Undo is the same accent text.
- **Icon button** (`.icon-btn`). 44px round, one 24px icon, an `aria-label`. For search, sort, ⋮ and the back arrow. A page's add action (Add podcast) is the plus in `accent` with no fill (`.accent-icon`). Use a text button when no familiar icon says the action.
- **Back** (`NestedHead`, `HeroBar`, `BackRow`). The arrow goes back, or to a fallback screen when the page was opened directly. `NestedHead` is a nested screen's whole head on one row: the arrow, the title (22 / 800, one line, "…" when long) with an optional sub line under it, and the actions on the right. `HeroBar` is the top row of a page with a hero (a book, show or movie): the arrow, and ⋮ on the right. `BackRow` is the arrow alone, for an artist's page and error states.
- **Text tabs** (`Chips kind="tabs"`, `.subtabs`; `TextTabs` in the app). Switch between views of the same content: Home / Authors / Series / Finished, Home / Shows / Browse on Podcasts, All and the categories on Top shows, Episodes / Unplayed / Bookmarks / More like on a show's page (search and sort at the right of the same row, tabs 20px apart there), Home / Liked / Playlists / Albums / Artists, Home / Movies / Shows / Watched, Week / Month / Year / All time, You's settings, Chapters / Bookmarks, Add songs' sources, Search's kinds, a show's seasons, and the tabs in edit sheets. Every view switch is a text tab; chips are only for settings. Labels are 15 / 700 in `muted`; the selected one is `text` with a 2px `accent` underline over a `line` hairline. They sit under the page title, 24px apart, and scroll sideways when they don't fit: a finger swipes, and a mouse drags or turns the wheel. The row fades out over 48px at an end that has more to scroll to (`data-more`). A selected tab past an edge scrolls into view clear of the fade: already there when the page opens on it, gliding when it's picked. The underline slides to the tab picked (about 280ms; already in place when the page opens), and the view under the tabs fades in. One selected, never an action.
- **Chip** (`Chips`, `Chip`; `.chip`, `.chips`). Turns fields on and off one by one (`toggle`: the fields Match all takes), or picks one in a few places inside a sheet (`radio`). Never switches views; that's a text tab. Settings on You don't use chips: a choice is a row that opens a choice sheet. Admin lists (Needs attention, Not matched) aren't chips either: they're rows in You > Library. On Not matched, Match all is a text action in the title row, with no count line under it; once results are in, their tabs (To review, Applied, No match) and Apply stick to the top while the list scrolls.
- **Badge** (`CountPill`, `SectionTitle`; `.count-pill`, `.badge-count`). Grey counts what's in a list, New episodes and New albums included; accent flags something new or waiting. No count next to a screen's title, nested or not. `SectionTitle` is a section heading with its count, as a button when it opens the full list; a long one ends in "…" and keeps its count. A group's head (`GroupGrid`, Authors and Series) puts its second line under it: the author at the left and a note like "1 finished" at the right, which keeps its width.
- **Search** (`SearchView`; `app/(tabs)/search.tsx` in the app). One search for everything, opened from the Search tab. Under the "Search" title, a pill-shaped box (placeholder "Search") stays at the top while the results scroll; it takes focus when Search opens with nothing typed, has an `accent` ring while focused, and an X to clear once there's text. Once there are results, text tabs sit under the box, above them: All, then only the kinds (Books, Podcasts, Music, Watch, those switched on) that have matches, and no tabs at all when fewer than two kinds do. A tab left with nothing goes back to All. While the next answer loads, the last tabs stay so they don't blink. All shows each group (Books, Authors, Series, Podcasts, Episodes, Artists, Albums, Songs, Movies and shows, TV episodes) as a shelf of its usual cards, or 4 of its usual rows, under a heading that opens its kind's tab; a kind's tab shows grids and full lists. No count pills: the groups are capped. With the box empty, a Recent heading lists the last 8 searches, each with an X; a search is kept when it's submitted or a result is opened. Offline, the app searches what's downloaded and says "Showing downloaded only". No results reads `Nothing matches "<query>".` everywhere.
- **Library title** (`LibraryTitle`, `.library-title`). Library's sections side by side at page-title size, always in Tabs order, the current one `text` and the others `faint`. Tapping one switches; there's no swiping between sections, so up-and-down scrolling and the shelves have every drag. The title row and the section's text tabs stay at the top while the content scrolls under them (`.library-head` on the web), and stay still while switching. Podcasts and Search keep their title, tabs and Search's box up the same way (`StickyHead`, `.sticky-head`); Browse's box sticks right under Podcasts' tabs; only the content below fades in. On phones (420px and under) it's 28px with 12px gaps so all three fit beside one button, and it scrolls sideways if it still doesn't. With one section left it's a plain title.
- **Like** (`LikeButton`, `.like-btn`). A heart, music only: outline in `muted`, filled `accent` once liked. On every song row, on the album page as a round button after Shuffle, and in the full player next to a song's title.
- **Search box** (`SearchBox`). For finding inside one screen's own list (Add songs, a show's episodes): opens under the page head, focused, with a placeholder that says what it searches.
- **Detail header** (`.hero`, `.book-hero`). Books, podcasts, albums, playlists, movies and TV shows share one header: the cover's tint and blurred art behind it, back and ⋮ alone on the top row, then the cover centred (a 2:3 poster for movies and shows), with the text centred under it. Above the title, in accent, is the series (a book) or collection (a movie); "Playlist" goes there in `text-2`. Then the title, then the author or artist as an accent link, or a podcast's author in `text-2`. Last is one muted facts line (`.book-meta`: 1980, 10 songs, 42m), spaced apart with no pills or dots. From 900px the cover sits on the left with the text beside it. Play goes in the actions row under it. A show you follow has a 32px round check on the cover's top right corner (`SubscribedCheck`, `.cover-corner`): white on a dark disc, and it asks before unsubscribing. A show you don't follow (its preview) has a primary Subscribe button in the actions row instead.
- **Pill** (`.pill`, `.pills`). Short technical facts in a box (a video file's codecs). Headers don't use them. Not clickable.
- **Cover** (`Cover` in `web/cover.tsx`). Square artwork with a tinted fallback that shows the title. Artists are round; playlists with four or more albums show a 2×2 grid. No borders, no text over real artwork.
- **Card** (`.card`, `.grid`, `.shelf`). A cover with a title and one sub line, straight on `bg`. A started book adds a progress bar; a finished one gets the accent check. Books, albums, podcasts and playlists share one size: grids are 3 across and shelves 2.5 across on phones. Continue listening cards are about a quarter bigger and show the title, author and time left. Podcasts' Continue listening cards are wide instead (`.pod-continue`, 290px so the next one peeks in): a 14px `surface` card mixed with 15% of the show's colour, a 64px cover beside the show name and two-line episode title, then the episode row's play pill and progress bar. Book, album, movie, show, artist, playlist and podcast cards, Continue listening and Continue watching included, all open their item's menu the same way (`CardMenu` in `web/card-menu.tsx`): ⋮ in the top right corner or a right-click on the web, a long press with a haptic in the app. A book's menu reads Play, Add to Play next, Mark as finished, Select (wherever book cards are), Hide, then after a divider the admin items Edit…, Match…, Mark as matched and, last in `danger`, Remove from library…. The ⋮ on the item's page opens the same items in a sheet (`MenuSheet`), for everyone. Podcasts' wide Continue listening cards have no menu: their play, queue and played buttons are already on the card.
- **Selecting several** (`SelectionProvider`, `PickDisc`, `.select-bar` in `web/library/selection.tsx`; `pickRange`, `bulkChanges` and the rest in shared `bulk.ts`). Starts from Select in a card's menu, with that card picked, wherever book cards are: Library's Home shelves, Authors, Series and Finished, All books, a shelf, an author, a series. Continue listening cards can't be picked. A book on two shelves is picked on both, and switching tabs leaves select mode. While selecting, a tap or click picks a card instead of opening it, shift-click picks the run from the last one, and Esc, the bar's X or back leaves. Each cover shows a disc where the finished check goes: empty (a white ring on a dark disc), or `accent` with a check and an `accent` outline on the cover once picked. On wide screens the bar is a `surface` strip that sticks to the top over the back row (or Library's title row, which hides under it), so nothing moves: X, "5 selected" (15 / 700; "0 selected" in `muted` 600) and All (Select all, short so a phone's bar fits) on the left, then text actions on the right, words alone with no icons: Edit… (admins), Mark as finished (Mark as not finished when every pick is finished), Hide, and Remove… in red (admins). The actions grey out with nothing picked. On a phone it's the same one bar at the top, a little tighter, with the actions as 22px `accent` icons on the right: Finished and More (Hide, then Edit… and Remove from library… for admins), with Download between them in the app. The mini player stays where it is. Mark as finished and Hide confirm with a toast with Undo, which puts each book back as it was.
- **Bulk edit** (`BulkEditSheet`). Edit on several books: the picked covers stacked (up to 3) beside "Edit 5 books", then only the fields books share (Author, Narrator, Series; never series numbers or years). A field the books differ on is blank with "Mixed" as its placeholder and, left blank, keeps each book's own value. A changed field gets the Edited tag and Undo, and one full-width "Save 2 changes" saves (`saveLabel`). Series keeps each book's own number.
- **Scroll row** (`ScrollRow`, web only). Wraps a shelf so a mouse gets round arrows at its ends while hovering, each hidden once that end is reached. Touch screens just swipe.
- **Shelves.** A section title over a shelf of cards; an empty shelf isn't shown at all, and one that can't be built is left off without touching the rest of the page. "More like" names its item ("More like Perelandra"); Customize lists it under that title too. Short listens' cards show the length as their sub line. Music Home stacks Jump back in (album and playlist cards), On repeat (five song rows; picking one queues the whole list of up to 25), More like *album* (album cards), More like *artist* (artist cards: a round photo, else one of their covers, else a person icon, with the name and album count centred under it, `.artist-card`) and New albums (album cards, with a grey count). On a movie page the collection shelf marks the current film with an accent title (`.card-current`).
- **Subtitle line** (`.vp-subs`). White 600 text on a 62% black box with 6px corners, one box per line, centred at the bottom of the picture and sized to it (13px on a phone held upright, about 20px sideways). It moves up above the controls when they show. The Subtitles tile fills with `accent` while subtitles are on.
- **New tile** (`.new-tile`). Making something new from a grid is its first tile: a cover-sized `surface-2` square (the cover placeholder colour, so it reads in both themes) with an accent plus and no outline, its name below as the card title. Used for New playlist.
- **Mini player sub line** (`.mini-split`). A book's chapter and time left share one line; the chapter name gives way first.
- **List row** (`.list-row`, `.queue-row`; `SongRow` for songs). Thumbnail, bold title, one muted sub line, trailing controls (length, ⋮ menu, drag handle). The left part is the tap target. The playing row's title is `accent`. Lists dragged into order use `useSortable` and its `DragHandle` (in `web/sortable.tsx`), which also moves a row with the arrow keys.
- **Track list** (`.track-list`, `.chapter-list`). Numbered rows for an album's songs or a book's chapters, numbers right-aligned and tabular, no thumbnails.
- **Mini player** (`.mini`). Cover, title, sub line, transport and a 2px accent progress line. Books and episodes get skip back 10 / forward 30; songs get previous / next, never speed or skip-seconds.
- **Bottom bar** (`.tabs`). Search, Library, Podcasts and You, those shown, sharing the width equally, each a 24px icon over an 11px label; the selected one is `accent`. Search is an ordinary tab. Library opens the section you were last in and is selected on all of them. The keyboard covers the bar. Settings live on You, not in the bar.
- **Sheet** (`Sheet`, `SheetItem` in `web/sheet.tsx`). Rises from the bottom for item menus, one- or two-field forms and lists like the queue. Items are verbs; a danger item goes last. Rows have no lines between them, only a highlight on hover, press or keyboard focus (no focus ring), and the content scrolls without a scrollbar; the one divider sits before admin items. Close it before acting and confirm with a toast. No browser dialogs. A sheet can carry a grey count, a sub line and one action (like Clear) in its title row; the title row stays put and only the content under it scrolls, at most 85% of the screen tall. Edit sheets (a book, a movie or show) look alike: a small cover and "Edit book", "Edit movie" or "Edit show" over the name, text tabs (a book's Details, Cover, Chapters, Match, Files; a movie's or show's Details, Poster, Background, Match), an Undo by each field with unsaved typing, picture tabs (`PicturePicker` in `web/picture-picker.tsx`) and one full-width Save on the tabs it saves (Details, and a book's Chapters), greyed out until something changes, then saying how much ("Save 2 changes", `saveLabel`); there's no Close button, since the sheet closes like any other. Nothing applies on a tap: a suggested picture or a Match result is picked first, and one full-width button in the sheet's foot then applies it ("Use this picture", "Use this match", a book's "Review 3 changes" (`reviewLabel`) and then "Apply 3 fields"), held there while the list scrolls above it. With nothing picked, no button shows. Match's Search is a plain button, and results load as skeleton cards, not a spinner. On a phone a sheet has a grab bar at the top: dragging it or the title row down moves the sheet with the finger and fades the backdrop, and letting go past a third of the way, or with a flick, closes it; otherwise it springs back. Escape, the backdrop and a drag all close it the same way, sinking out of sight. Edit, Match and Change photo are sheets like any other: the same grab bar, drag, sink and no Close button (`useSheetDialog` in `web/sheet.tsx` gives a custom <dialog> all of it). While any sheet is open on a phone, the page behind it doesn't scroll.
- **Picture tab** (`PicturePicker`). A book's Cover and a movie's or show's Poster and Background. The current picture with "Current", where it's from ("Custom cover", "From the files", "From TMDB") and, when it's your own, an accent "Use the files' cover" (or "Use TMDB's picture"). Then a CHOOSE ANOTHER eyebrow over a grid whose first tile is Upload (`surface-2` with an accent plus; click, or drop on wide screens) and then the suggestions, each labelled by where it's from (Audible, Google Books, Apple Books; TMDB's have no label). The picked one gets an `accent` outline, the check disc and an `accent` label. Under the grid, "Paste an image link" sits on a hairline with a link icon; Enter applies it. "Use this picture" sits full width at the foot once something is picked. On a phone the current picture is 132px beside its text, with 3 columns below; wider, it's 220px on the left with 4 columns on the right.
- **Player controls** (`.player-controls`). Books and episodes: previous chapter, skip back, play, skip forward, next chapter. An episode without chapters (most of them) uses the outer pair for previous and next instead: the item before or after it in the queue, then the episode above or below it in its show's list. Songs: Repeat, previous, play, next, Shuffle. Shuffle and Repeat are muted icon buttons that turn `accent` when on; Repeat one adds a small 1 to the loop.
- **Playing mixer** (`Mixer`, `.mixer`). Five accent bars bouncing out of step on a dark disc, centered on the cover of the book, album, playlist or show that's playing; the card's title turns `accent`. Paused, the bars ease from where they were into the app icon's shape (full, ¾, about half, ¾, full) and stay there. The playing song's thumbnail in playlist and Songs rows gets the thumbnail version. In an album's track list, the bars alone (`MixerBars`, accent, no disc) take the playing song's number. On the playing row in the queue it fills the thumbnail and shows Play while paused, since tapping that row pauses and resumes.
- **Queue rows** (`web/up-next.tsx`). One running list: played rows in `faint` with a dimmed thumbnail, the playing row with the mixer and an `accent` title, then what's next. Each row: thumbnail, title (`Marquee`: scrolls sideways when it doesn't fit, ellipsis with reduced motion), artist or show with the length beside it, then X and the drag handle. No section labels. Reduced motion stops the bars.
- **Volume row** (`VolumeRow`, `.vol-row`). The web audio player's volume, under the controls on desktop: a mute button showing the soft speaker, a slider, and the loud speaker at the far end, so the title keeps its full width. Hidden on touch screens. The video player keeps its speaker button that slides a slider out on hover.
- **Slider thumbs** (`.hover-thumb`). Seek and volume bars show only the bar and its fill at rest; the round thumb grows in (about 120ms) while you hover, press or tab to the bar, and in the app while a finger is down. Speed sliders keep theirs.
- **Player tiles** (`.tile`). Speed, Sleep, a third slot, and Queue with a grey count; each opens its control in place or in a sheet. The slot (`thirdTile` in shared) is Chapters for anything with more than one chapter, else Bookmarks. Chapters' sheet has Chapters and Bookmarks text tabs, and its title and grey count follow the tab. The order never changes, and the slot is decided when the item loads so nothing pops in mid-listen. The ad setting sits in the episode's ⋮ menu as a row with its value and a chevron.
- **Bookmark rows** (`BookmarkRows`, `.bookmark`). One row everywhere: a round `surface-2` play button, the time as the title (15 / 700, tabular), the chapter if there is one, the note if there is one in `text-2`, and a pencil. No "No note" text. Sorted by time. An episode row with bookmarks gets a small `accent` bookmark beside its date, as `.ads-checked` does. The bookmark beside the player's title fills within 15 seconds of one. On the scrubber they're `accent` ticks taller than the bar (`.scrub-bookmarks`), over played, unplayed and ad marks alike and under the thumb.
- **Chapter scrubber** (`.scrub-segs`, `.scrub-seg`). An episode with chapters gets one segment per chapter across the whole bar, with small gaps between: played ones solid, the current one filling, the rest faint, with ad marks on top. The playing chapter's title takes the middle of the times row in place of the time left (`.player-chapter-line`, 12 / 700, ellipsis; tap for the list). While dragging, the current segment thickens and the times row shows the title of the chapter under the thumb in `accent`. On the web, hovering with a mouse does the same for the spot under the pointer, and a later chapter's segment lights up in `accent` (`.scrub-seg.hovered`). A book's bar spans its current chapter, with the chapter's name as the line under the title (the Chapters tile opens the list).
- **Chapter list** (`ChapterList`, `.chapter-list`). Number, title on one line with an ellipsis, and length. Played rows are muted, and the current row has an `accent` title, with `MixerBars` in place of its number. The sheet title carries a grey count. Video has no speed: its tiles are Audio, Pop out and Full screen.
- **Floating card** (`.float-card`). A short note with one `accent` text action and an X, floating over a player so nothing moves when it comes and goes; it fades in and stays dismissed until what it's about changes. Used for the video player's "Converting slower than it plays", just above the bottom tiles, and the audio player's Go back after a jump to a bookmark, just below the top bar, where it can't cover the controls on a short screen. Anything that stays up while you listen on the audio player uses a cover pill instead, since a phone has little room there.
- **Cover pill** (`.skip-ad`, `.finish-pill`). A small pill in the cover's bottom-right corner for a timely one-tap action, so the controls never move: Skip ad (ad colours) while an ad plays, and Mark played (`accent`, with a check; both icons sit after the text) in an episode's last 3 minutes. When both apply they stack (`.cover-pills`), Mark played on top. Skip ad goes once less than a second is left, since an ad that runs to the end has nothing to skip to.
- **Player top row** (`.player-top`). Close on the left, the "Playing from Books" eyebrow centred ("Finished" once a book has stopped at its end), and the cast button and ⋮ menu on the right.
- **Cast button** (`CastButton`). Google's cast mark, only there when a Chromecast is found; `accent` with its screen filled while casting. While casting, the eyebrow reads "Playing on Living Room", the mini player's sub line is a small accent cast mark and the device name, and the video stage shows the backdrop at 45% with a white cast mark.
- **Settings** (`SettingsGroup`, `Setting`, `SettingSub`, `SettingLink`, `ChoiceRow`, `SwitchRow`; `.set-group`, `.setting`; `SETTINGS_TABS` in shared). Settings live on You, under the stats card and a Settings heading (`.you-heading`, 22 / 800, the same as the Stats heading in the card), as text tabs (`Chips kind="tabs"`, like Library's views, so on a phone they scroll sideways and fade at an edge with more): Playback, Appearance, Podcasts, Account, then Library, Users and Server for admins. `/you/<tab>` opens one, and switching stays on the page. No panels, pills, chips or status dots: flat rows split by `line` hairlines, under eyebrow group labels (12px uppercase `muted`, 36px above each group). Each row has a title (15 / 600) and an optional `muted` sub line on the left. On the right, a choice shows its value and a chevron and opens a choice sheet (`ChoiceRow`); on/off says "On" in `accent` or "Off" in `muted`, lined up with the values (`SwitchRow`, a hidden checkbox underneath); an action is a text action with its icon. A failure is a red sub line; something waiting is an `accent` count (`badge-count`) by the chevron; an empty admin list gets no row. Rescan always carries the refresh icon, which spins while the scan runs. While a scan runs, its row says how far it is ("Scanning, 412 of 1,210 songs", `scanStatusText`), with a 3px `accent` bar on `track` across the whole row under it (`ScanBar` in `web/scan-status.tsx`), the same on the first-run library step. Account starts with a "User" row showing the username, and Sign out is a lone red row that asks first. Groups are 36px apart; a group with no label, like Sign out, only 16px. The last row's own padding counts toward the page's bottom space, so the tabs end the same distance above the tab bar as every other page. Server has the version and backups, the Android app download for this version, and the server address with Copy. What you hid is its own nested page from Account.
- **Choice sheet** (`ChoiceSheet`, web). A sheet of a few choices, like a sort or a setting's value. The current one is marked, and picking closes it.
- **Confirm sheet** (`ConfirmSheet`, web and app). Asks before something can't be undone: a title ending in a question mark ("Delete Road trip?", "Remove 5 books?"), one line on what happens, then the action and Cancel: solid `danger` (`.btn-danger-fill`) for removing something, primary for signing out. On a phone it's centred, with what it's about (up to 3 covers, stacked) above the title, and the action full width over a plain Cancel; on a wide screen it's a small dialog with Cancel and the action on the right. Used for signing out, removing a user, deleting a playlist, unsubscribing, and Remove from library (one book or several; it says the files stay on the server and the next scan adds them back unless you move them).
- **Toast** (`Toaster`, sonner; `toast` from `web/toast.tsx`). Comes in from the top and says what just happened ("Hid 5 books", "Removed Piranesi from the library"). An undoable action adds Undo (or Open) as `accent` text on the right, never a pill: always import `toast` from `web/toast.tsx`, which swaps sonner's own pill action for a `.text-btn` and keeps that toast up for 5 seconds.
- **Form controls** (`.input`, `.switch`, checkboxes). 44px inputs on `surface` with a `line` border that turns `accent` on focus. Placeholders say what to type ("Playlist name"). Each form has one primary button.
- **Stats** (`StatsCard`, `StatsPage`, `.stats-*`; the rules are in `stats-page.ts` in shared). On You, one card: a Stats heading with the chevron on its row, then this week's eyebrow, total and comparison, and a sparkline. The card opens Stats, a nested screen with Week, Month, Year and All time text tabs, each fading in.
  - **Sparkline** (`.stats-spark`). One 2.5px `accent` line with a faint fill and a 9px dot at today, both the same size however wide the card is, and a `track` line for the days to come. Under it (`.stats-axis`), the first and last day, and Month's 15th, in small `muted` text, and Today in `text`, which takes the place of any label it would crowd. Month's is a rolling average.
  - **Sentences.** The lead is Stats total type with the main kind in its colour. Week's rows are the day, a cover, what you played most and the time, with Today in `accent`. Month's are a cover, a sentence in 16 / 700 and a chevron. Rows part with `line` hairlines.
  - **Ring** (`.stats-ring`). A spoke per day, 2.2 wide with round caps, in `stat-book` (the accent), `stat-episode`, `stat-music` or `stat-watch` by the week's main kind; `track` stubs for days with nothing played and days to come; a `text` line at today; the total in the middle and Jan, Apr, Jul and Oct around it, up to 440 wide. Year has the legend below it, which is also the filter: picking a kind outlines it in its colour, fades the others and turns the ring, total and Top to that kind. All time has year tiles below it instead (`.stats-years`): 44 high, sharing the row and scrolling sideways once there are too many to fit, with the picked one in `accent`.
  - **Top** (`.stats-top-strip`). The period's top things as square covers with the kind as a small uppercase label, scrolling sideways on a phone and a grid with room.
  - A hidden tab leaves its kind out everywhere, the total included; with only books left there's no legend. With no listening at all, the screen is just its empty state.
- **Episode rows** (`.episode-row`). A 16:9 still with a progress bar, then the title over "S1 E2, 31m" in a smaller muted line, both sitting on the still's bottom edge, and a watched toggle on the right. The video player's Next episodes list uses the same row and scrolls with a thin bar and no arrows; on short windows the picture shrinks so a row and a half still show.
- **Directory shows** (`DirectoryCard`, `DirectoryRow` in `web/podcasts/browse.tsx`; `.dir-*`). A show you don't follow looks and works the same in every Browse list. A card has the subscribe button on the cover's bottom right (`.dir-corner`), clear of the ⋮ on the top right: a white heart on a dark disc, then an `accent` disc with a check once followed, which opens the show. A row has the same toggle at its end as a 44px icon button (`.dir-heart`: a `muted` heart, then an `accent` check), with the rank before the thumbnail on charts (`.dir-rank`, 15 / 800, tabular, `muted`). The show itself opens its preview. Category tiles (`.dir-genre`) are 64px `surface` boxes with the name in 15 / 700, two across on phones.
- **Hidden list** (You > Hidden; `HiddenList`). Books, Music and Watch sections, each a `SectionTitle` with a grey count, of list rows (round thumb for artists) ending in an "Unhide" button. Empty sections are left out; with none, "Nothing hidden".
- **Empty state** (`EmptyState`; `.empty`, `.music-empty`). One bold line and one muted sentence, no illustrations, no blame.
